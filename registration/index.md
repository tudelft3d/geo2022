Responsible supervisors use this page to register the date, time and room of the Kick-off and Green-light assessments of their students, as described in the [Graduation Guide](../rules/) (the Finalisation is scheduled separately via SuperSaaS or e-mail to `graduation-bk@tudelft.nl`).

## Registering an assessment

1. Agree on a date and time with the student, other supervisor(s) and delegate (if already appointed), picking one of the standard [time slots](#time-slots) before the [registration deadline](../dates/). As the responsible supervisor, book a room for that time slot.
2. Register the assessment using the appropriate form below (you will be asked to sign in with your TU Delft account):
   - [Kick-off registration form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=TVJuCSlpMECM04q0LeCIe_rGY9YbKDFBlS8d-oCeONpUMklFMkU2VEs3RE1TTVhCUDhKSjVTTFBERS4u)
   - [Green-light registration form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=TVJuCSlpMECM04q0LeCIe_rGY9YbKDFBlS8d-oCeONpUQzlWODdaSVpFMVpURk9JSVo3MzBHMTVHOC4u)
3. That is all. The course coordinator checks the registrations, appoints the delegate, and then sends a calendar invitation to everyone involved (the student, both supervisors and the delegate) ahead of the assessment. If anyone else is attending as well (for example the co-reader), simply forward them the invitation.

## Changing or cancelling

* If an already registered assessment needs to move to a different date or time:
  * and no delegate has been appointed, just edit the submitted form.
  * and a delegate has already been appointed, make sure that everyone is available (delegate included) and e-mail `graduation-bk@tudelft.nl` with the new details (date, time and room). Please put the coordinator in cc.
* If an already registered Kick-off assessment is cancelled, the student or supervisors should e-mail the coordinator and delegate (if appointed). The responsible supervisor should also withdraw it in MyCase.
* If an already registered Green-light assessment is cancelled, the student or supervisors should e-mail the coordinator, delegate and `graduation-bk@tudelft.nl`.
