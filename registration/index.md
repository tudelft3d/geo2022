Responsible supervisors will be able to use this page to register the date and time slot of the Kick-off and Green-light assessments of their students, as described in the Graduation Guide. The registration form is not available yet.

Until then, request an assessment date and time slot by email to `graduation-bk@tudelft.nl`, picking a free slot from the [graduation calendar](../dates/).
