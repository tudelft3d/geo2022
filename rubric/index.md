_This is the rubric used to assess the thesis at the Green light and the Finalisation. The committee gives one grade per category (half-points allowed); the aspects within each category are a checklist to support that judgment — you can click the descriptor that best matches each aspect to get an advisory average per category, then choose the category grade yourself. For feedback to put in MyCase, you can fill in the form below, download it as a PDF and upload it to MyCase. If you're a delegate for an upcoming Green light or Finalisation, you can download the grade list [here](grade-list.pdf). For the Kick-off, use the [kick-off rubric](plan/) instead._
