_This is the rubric used to assess the thesis at the Green light and the Finalisation. The aspects within each category are a checklist to support the committee's judgment — click the descriptor that best matches each aspect to get an advisory average per category, then enter the final grade for each category in the summary. Descriptors are at whole grades (the “< 5.75” column counts as a 5 in the advisory average), while final grades may be non-whole marks. For feedback to put in MyCase, you can fill in the form below, download it as a PDF and upload it to MyCase. If you're a delegate for an upcoming Green light or Finalisation, you can download the grade list [here](grade-list.pdf). For the Kick-off, use the [kick-off rubric](plan/) instead._
