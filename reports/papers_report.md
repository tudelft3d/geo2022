# Paper cross-check (2026-09-29)

The archive holds 314 theses, 32 of which already link a paper. The other 280 were compared against the GDMC publication list (838 scientific/conference papers), Crossref (searched per thesis on title and student author) and OpenAlex works of their supervisors and students, scoring candidates on co-authorship, title similarity and abstract similarity.

Accepted papers go into the entry's `paper:` field in _data/geotheses.yml (optionally `paper_label:`), several at once as a `papers:` list of {url, label} entries; rejected candidates go into scripts/verified_papers.yml with `verdict: not related`, and a thesis to skip entirely with `verdict: no papers`.
